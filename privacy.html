Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what data the DramaRecap service accesses and how it is handled.

Data we access

  • Your TikTok basic profile information (username, avatar) required to connect your account.
  • Permission to create and publish videos on your behalf, only when you authorize the connection.

How data is used

  • Access tokens are stored locally on your own machine and are never sold or shared with third parties.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies are used.
  • Uploaded video files remain on your machine and are sent to TikTok only when a scheduled upload runs.

Your rights

  • You can revoke TikTok access at any time from your TikTok settings or by contacting us.
